[center][b]Gravity Tools[/b][/center]

A mod adding gravity manipulation equipment for Delta-V.

[u][b]Features:[/b][/u]

[b]Gravity Beacon Drone[/b]
[indent]A deployable drone that creates localized gravity wells to attract or repel objects within its radius.[/indent]

[b]Gravity Gun[/b]
[indent]A high-stress mounted "weapon" that projects a directional gravity field. Pulls objects towards your ship within a cone.[/indent]
